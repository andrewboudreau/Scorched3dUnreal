# Sounds in this folder

All CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
so they may be redistributed. Each was trimmed, converted to mono and
peak-normalised, and is shipped as `.ogg` (Opus) with an `.mp3` fallback.
`src/audio.js` (`DEFAULT_SAMPLES`, `CUES`) and `src/sfxmap.js` say what plays when.
